Care & Maintenance

How to Care for Your Piece

Cleaning

Hover to learn more ?

Wipe with a soft, damp cloth. Avoid abrasive scrubbers and harsh chemical cleaners that can dull the high-gloss surface.

UV Exposure

Hover to learn more ?

Our UV-stabilized epoxy resists yellowing for 5+ years in indirect light. Avoid prolonged direct outdoor UV exposure.

Heat & Moisture

Hover to learn more ?

Use coasters under hot cups - epoxy is heat-resistant to 60°C. Do not place hot pots directly on resin surfaces.
