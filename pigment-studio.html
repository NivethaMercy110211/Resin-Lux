Pairing Rules

The Art of Colour Pairing

For a high-end premium look, we recommend selecting one dominant shade, one accent colour, and one special effect. Too many competing colours can reduce the clarity and impact of the final piece.

Dominant - 60%

The base colour that defines the overall mood. Oceanic Teal, Midnight Galaxy, Rose Quartz.

Accent - 30%

A complementary colour that adds depth. Liquid Gold pairs beautifully with Oceanic Teal.

Effect - 10%

Crystal shimmer, glow-in-dark or metallic flake - used sparingly for maximum impact.

Special Effects

Enhance Your Colour with Effects

Glow in the Dark

Strontium aluminate pigment that glows for 8+ hours. Best for statement tables and feature walls.

Transparent Tint

Crystal-clear resin with a subtle hint of colour — best for jewelry, pendants and small accent pieces.

Metallic Swirl

Gold or silver mica swirled through the pour — creates dimensional depth in tables and serving trays.

Wave Pour

Dual-pigment wave technique where two shades intersect naturally — creates an organic ocean-wave effect.

Gold Leaf Flakes

Real 24k gold leaf suspended mid-pour for luxurious, irregular flakes of genuine gold in the resin layer.

Botanical Embedding

Dried flowers, ferns or botanicals preserved permanently within UV crystal resin — each piece unique.

Artisan Tip

Less is More — When It Counts

For a high-end, gallery-quality look: one dominant pigment, one metallic accent, and no more than one special effect. This is the formula behind our most celebrated commissions.
