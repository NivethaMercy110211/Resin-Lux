Production Steps

How Each Service Is Made

You share dimensions, references and pigment preferences via our commission form. We follow up with a digital design mockup within 48 hours for your approval before any resin is poured.

Timber slabs are planed, sanded and sealed. Epoxy is measured to gram precision, pigments are pre-mixed. Your workbench mold is prepared and levelled — accuracy is paramount before the first drop of resin.

Resin is poured in precision layers, allowing each layer to partially cure before the next. The full cure cycle runs 7 days — and every 12 hours, your dashboard updates with fresh stage photos and telemetry.

Post-cure, surfaces are wet-sanded from 320→10,000 grit for a mirror finish. Our lead artisan performs a full visual and structural inspection before marking the piece as delivery-ready.

White-glove packaging with foam-inlay custom boxes. You'll receive a dashboard notification to schedule your studio pickup or confirm delivery logistics. Final photos are shared in your gallery on delivery.
