Our Origin Story

Where Wood Meets Water,
Where Art Meets Home

ResinLux Studio was born from a simple conviction: everyday objects deserve to be extraordinary. What began as a weekend passion project in a Chennai garage has grown into a studio renowned for bespoke river tables, jewelry and corporate gifting.

Every piece that leaves our studio is a one-of-a-kind collaboration — guided by client input at every stage, from pigment selection to pickup scheduling.

Pure Inputs

Premium Materials, Zero Compromises

Quality materials are the foundation of clarity, strength and longevity in every piece.

Crystal-Clear Epoxy

UV-Stable · Food-Safe

Our premium two-part epoxy resin is UV-stabilised and food-grade certified. Zero yellowing is guaranteed for 5 years, even under direct sunlight exposure.

Yellowing-free for 5 Years — UV clarity guarantee

Artist-Grade Pigments

52+ Shades · Hand-Mixed

We use mica, pearl, and metallic pigments mixed in-house for richer, deeper colour depth. Each batch is weighed to the milligram for consistent results across pieces.

Over 52 pigment & shimmer combinations available

Kiln-Dried Timber

FSC-Certified · Live-Edge

Seasoned walnut, golden oak and dark ash slabs sourced exclusively from FSC-certified suppliers. Every slab is kiln-dried to <8% moisture, ensuring structural stability over time.

Sourced from FSC-Certified sustainable forest suppliers

Diamond Polish Process

10K Grit · Mirror Gloss

We wet-sand each surface through 12 progressive grits — from 320 to 10,000 — before applying a final machine buff. The result is a mirror gloss that rivals optical glass.

Wet diamond sanding: 320→10,000 grit mirror finish
