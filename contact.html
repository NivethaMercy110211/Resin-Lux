Quick Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I visit the studio without an appointment?
Yes, walk-ins are welcome Monday–Saturday during opening hours. For private consultations or pickup scheduling, we recommend booking ahead via your dashboard or WhatsApp.
How quickly will I receive a reply to my message?
We respond to all messages within 4 hours during studio hours (Mon–Sat, 10am–7pm). WhatsApp is typically faster for quick queries — responses within 30 minutes during peak hours.
Do you offer home delivery across India?
Yes — we ship to most major cities using trusted freight partners for large furniture, and courier services for smaller pieces. Shipping costs are quoted per order based on destination and package dimensions.
Can I visit the studio to see finished pieces in person?
Absolutely — our studio showroom is open Mon–Sat and displays a rotating selection of completed commissions. It's a great way to see the glass-like finish and colour depth in real life before commissioning.
