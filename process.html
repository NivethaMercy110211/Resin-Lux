Thermal Stability

Curing Matters

Curing is the heart of epoxy art. It isn't just about setting; it is a complex exothermic chemical reaction. Our specialized curing chamber maintains a steady 24°C and 45% relative humidity, ensuring the resin achieves maximum crystal clarity, structural stability, and a rock-hard rating of 80 Shore D.

Production Timeline

Crafting Scale & Timelines

The timeline for epoxy casting depends heavily on the volume of resin and depth of the wood slabs. Larger volumes require slow-curing resin formulas to prevent thermal shock and maintain absolute glass-like clarity.

River Tables: 4-6 Weeks Jewelry/Coasters: 7-10 Days Wall Art: 2-3 Weeks
Micro Pieces

Coasters & Pendants

Fast, single-layer casting. Uses high-viscosity resin that cures within 24-48 hours. Minimal preparation and rapid high-grit mechanical polish.

Grand Scale

Bespoke River Tables

Requires wood kiln drying, flattening, multiple deep pours over weeks, a specialized 7-day cure phase, and comprehensive water-sanding up to 10,000 grit.
