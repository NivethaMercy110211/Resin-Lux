The Craft

Techniques Behind Every Piece

Each creation method is chosen carefully to serve the final aesthetic and function.

River Casting

Slow-pour layers of pigmented resin flow between two wood slabs, forming the characteristic river channel — each one unrepeatable.

Swirl & Marble

Multiple pigment shades are poured simultaneously and manipulated with tools to create organic, flowing marble patterns in coasters and trays.

Botanical Embedding

Dried flowers, leaves and gold foil are suspended mid-pour in UV-stable crystal resin, preserving their colour and form permanently.

Metallic Mica Pigments

Artist-grade mica powders create dimensional shimmer that shifts in quality depending on light source — from sunlight to candlelight.

Geode Artistry

Crushed crystal, mica and pigment form natural geode veins on flat surfaces — a signature style that mimics cross-sectioned gemstones.

Mirror Polish

Wet-sanding from 320→10,000 grit followed by polishing compounds creates a mirror surface that rivals flat glass.
