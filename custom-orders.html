FAQs

Common Commission Questions

How long does a commission take from brief to delivery?
Standard river tables take 10–14 days (7 days cure + preparation and polishing). Smaller items like coasters and jewelry take 5–7 days. You'll receive live dashboard updates throughout.
Is a deposit required upfront?
No payment is needed to receive your design mockup. Once you approve the mockup, a 30% deposit confirms production. The remaining 70% is due on delivery or pickup.
How many design revisions are included?
Every commission includes up to 3 free mockup revisions before any resin is poured. Additional revisions are available at ₹500 each. We always get it right within 3 rounds.
Can I supply my own timber slab?
Yes — we accept client-supplied slabs if they meet our moisture and flatness standards. Drop the slab at our studio for a free assessment before confirming your commission.
Do you deliver outside your city?
Yes, we ship across India using freight partners for large furniture. Shipping costs are calculated by weight and destination — share your pincode during commission and we'll include a delivery quote.
What if I'm not happy with the final piece?
We document the production process with photos at every stage. If the delivered piece doesn't match the approved mockup specifications, we will refinish or replace the affected section at no charge.
